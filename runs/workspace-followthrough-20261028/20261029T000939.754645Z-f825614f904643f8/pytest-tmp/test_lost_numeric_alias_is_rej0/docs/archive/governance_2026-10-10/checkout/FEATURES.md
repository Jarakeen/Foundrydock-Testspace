Poisoned exact-Health ticks and chronological status interaction effects remain explicit unresolved boundaries.

- **Phase 14 chronological status caster-state replay** — reviewed caster-side status consequences such as Sundered's temporary Weapon/Spell Damage now become sequence-aware windows and rebuild later saved-build DD contexts through the shared timed EffectVariant stat projection. ## Phase 15 — Encounter Team Verdict
- Added a read-only, Pydantic-hardened team-plan verdict over encounter/stage impact summaries.
- Verdicts are `beneficial`, `harmful`, `mixed`, `neutral`, `no_changes`, or `insufficient_evidence`.
- No weighted aggregate score is used: gains and losses remain visible by dimension, and incomplete/unresolved evidence blocks a definitive positive or negative verdict.
- Primary-role displacement is treated directionally: less displacement is favorable, more is unfavorable.


### Phase 15 encounter decision record
- Freezes the already-evaluated encounter recommendation entries, conflict-checked team plan, team impact summary, and team verdict into one immutable Pydantic review artifact.
- Rejects mixed encounter or Raid Stage provenance before a record can be produced.
- Preserves staged proposals, reasons, conflicts, unresolved evidence, verdict, and confidence without recomputing or mutating the Raid Plan.
- Intended as the stable review/audit payload for later Optimization UI and persistence work; current slice remains read-only.


### Phase 15 encounter approval packet
- Converts one immutable encounter decision record into a human-review packet without rerunning ranking, simulation, or recommendation logic.
- A deterministic SHA-256 digest binds any later approve/reject action to the exact frozen decision evidence that was reviewed.
- `beneficial`/`neutral` staged plans with complete evidence can become `ready`; conflicts, unresolved evidence, `insufficient_evidence`, `harmful`, or `mixed` verdicts remain blocked.
- Explicit rejection carries no apply proposals; explicit approval only releases the already-staged proposal tuple and still does not mutate a Raid Plan.


### Phase 15 approved apply bridge
- Adds a Phase 15-specific apply boundary that requires both the ready human-review packet and its matching explicit approval decision.
- Verifies the frozen decision-record digest and exact proposal tuple before delegating to the existing stale-plan-safe build-swap applier.
- Rejected decisions, blocked packets, altered proposal tuples, digest mismatches, and changed Raid Plan build/source state fail closed.
- Returns a revised immutable Raid Plan snapshot only; persistence remains caller-owned and is never automatic.


### Phase 15 approved Raid Plan persistence
- Persists only the exact Phase 15 build-swap tuple that survived decision review, explicit approval, and guarded apply.
- Requires the currently stored Raid Plan to equal the exact immutable snapshot reviewed by the user; any intervening plan edit fails closed.
- Creates a full `foundrydock.db` safety snapshot before the write and semantically verifies that the snapshot contains the exact reviewed Raid Plan.
- Saves through the canonical `RaidPlanRepository`, preserving its optimistic-concurrency check, revision history, Pydantic validation, and exact read-back verification.
- Returns a strict immutable persistence receipt; does not alter Saved Builds or `data/eso.db`.
